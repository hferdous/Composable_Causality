# Datasheet for DTCD

This datasheet follows Gebru et al., "Datasheets for Datasets" (Communications of the ACM, 2021). The same content appears as Appendix N of the paper.

## Motivation

**For what purpose was the dataset created?** DTCD was created to provide a configurable benchmark with documented identifiability status for evaluating time-series causal discovery and dynamic treatment-effect estimation methods, and to close the gap between performance on fixed synthetic benchmarks and robustness on real data.

**Who created the dataset?** Muhammad Hasan Ferdous and Md Osman Gani, Causal AI Lab, Department of Information Systems, University of Maryland, Baltimore County. ## Composition

**What do the instances represent?** Each instance is a multivariate time series X with T time steps and d variables, paired with its ground-truth contemporaneous and lagged adjacency matrices, the cached noise realization N, the observation mask R when the variant has missing values, and, for CATE cells, the counterfactual twin trajectory under a specified intervention.

**How many instances are there?** The reference benchmark has 9 graphs x 9 discovery variants x 10 seeds = 810 discovery instances per method (6,480 discovery rows for 8 methods) and 9 graphs x 2 CATE variants x 10 seeds = 180 CATE instances per estimator (720 rows for 4 estimators). The framework generates further instances on demand for any configuration.

**Spatio-temporal instances.** The framework also generates spatio-temporal grids of shape (T, L, d) for L locations on a configurable topology, paired with the spatial kernel matrix and the cached noise. These are not part of the reference benchmark and are available through `dtcd.spatial`.

**Are there sensitive attributes or personal data?** No. All data are synthetic.

## Collection process

**How was the data acquired?** Generated by the DTCD simulator according to the data-generating process documented in Section 3 of the paper. `generate_datasets.py` regenerates the reference benchmark exactly; structural weights are drawn from a seed derived from `md5(graph_id)`, so the same data are produced on any machine.

## Preprocessing, cleaning, labeling

Ground-truth adjacency matrices are produced jointly with the data; no post-hoc cleaning is performed. Splits are defined at the seed level. Seeds 42 to 51 are used for evaluation, and hyperparameters are tuned on a separate trajectory generated at seed 41, which is never evaluated.

## Uses

**Has the dataset been used already?** For benchmarking the methods in Section 4 of the paper.

**Are there tasks for which the dataset should not be used?** Strong performance on DTCD does not guarantee strong performance on real data. DTCD should not be the sole evidence for deployment decisions in safety-critical applications.
